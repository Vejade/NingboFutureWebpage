Own Brands

Five brands, one promise

From OEM excellence to brands of our own — each collection carries a distinct character for a distinct way of living.

LINKLIFE® — linking life’s finest details

LINKLIFE® is Ningbo Future’s demonstration of OEM solutions. At LINKLIFE® we wish the consumer can link the finest details of their everyday life, therefore enjoying it to the fullest.

L Selection® is the collection of Ningbo Future’s best-selling OEM products from the European market. At L Selection®, the MOQ is as low as 300 units. With its fashionable design and packaging, L Selection® empowers our LATAM clients with the opportunity to test the market at lowest cost and minimal risk.

Bonbon™ — a colourful candy jar

Bonbon™ means candy in French. We aim to build a fashionable concept collection corner with Bonbon™ — like a colourful candy jar, creating a variety of sweet flavours.

It provides fun and interesting home-life products and fashionable accessories that are affordable. Bonbon™ is the way of expressing the affordable yet exquisite lifestyle to the fullest.

Jake&Julia® — her elegance, his twist

Jake&Julia® is the collection of decorative accessories and small furniture. Entering the world of Jake&Julia®, you will experience the feeling of a warm welcome to their home — where the creative ideas from Julia have been mixed with the male influence of Jake.

Looking at the different items, you immediately see the “signature” of Julia, elegant with her feminine expression, finding beauty and happiness in even the smallest things. Between the lines, you will find a “little” twist of Jake, rough and cool, to give a last wink. Discover their world: Julia’s favourites mixed with Jake’s favourites.

ecovie® — eco living, hassle-free

ecovie® is Ningbo Future’s answer to a bright future. Healthiness and eco-friendliness shouldn’t be slogans for expensive marketing campaigns. The great cause of saving the Earth must be affordable and blend into our everyday lifestyle hassle-free.

At ecovie® we invite you to take action by substituting your everyday accessories with more eco-friendly ones.

Greemotion® — back to nature

Greemotion® brings the spirit of outdoor exploration into our everyday life. Guided by the beauty and miracle of nature, we design outdoor gadgets that are comfortable, stylish, and durable.

Our vision is simple — making reconnection with nature easy, comfortable, and affordable.
